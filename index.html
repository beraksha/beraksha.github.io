05 — Research

Academic work

Technical Seminar Report · Dept. of CSE, VKIT
Effects of IoT Communication Protocols for Precision Agriculture in Outdoor Environments

Compared Wi-Fi, ZigBee, and LoRa for outdoor smart-farming deployments across a one-acre test field, evaluating each on coverage, transmission efficiency, data integrity, power draw, and cost under clear and obstructed conditions. Wi-Fi came out ahead for high-bandwidth multimedia, LoRa proved the most energy-efficient and cost-efficient for long-range text data, and ZigBee landed in between for short-to-medium range use. Also proposed two data-recovery models: for single and multiple sensor failures; to improve overall network reliability. Submitted as part of my degree; not published externally.
